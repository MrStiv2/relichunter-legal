Privacy Policy

Updated: 19 October 2026

This policy describes Relic Hunter: Fossil Auction, published by Serhii Fedorenko / CampStudio. Contact: campstudiomail@gmail.com.

Game progress

Your excavation progress, inventory, currency, settings, purchases and subscription state are stored on your device. No CampStudio account or sign-in is required. The game does not upload your save files to a CampStudio server. Removing the app or clearing its storage may erase local progress and consumable currency. Platform backup services may separately back up app data according to your device settings.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for banner ads, optional rewarded ads and privacy choices. These services may collect approximate location derived from IP address, device and advertising identifiers, interactions with ads and the app, purchase-related signals, and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance. These data may be used for delivering and measuring advertising, personalization where permitted, analytics, fraud prevention and service operation. Depending on platform, region and your choices, identifiers may be used to track activity across other companies' apps and websites. Precise GPS location is not requested by the game.

Where applicable, a consent form is shown before advertising requests. On iOS, tracking permission is requested where required and eligible. You can change available advertising choices through the game's Privacy Options and device privacy settings. Declining tracking permission does not prevent gameplay. Ad availability and personalization depend on consent, platform settings and advertising providers. Google Privacy Policy · Google advertising information.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play through Unity In-App Purchasing. The game receives product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase receipts and subscription status to grant or restore entitlements and avoid duplicate rewards. CampStudio does not receive your payment card number. Store providers process payment and account information under their own privacy policies. Consumables and purchase history may be retained by the stores even if local game data is deleted.

Other platform services

Optional local notifications are scheduled on your device and can be disabled in system settings. Native review requests and external links use the operating system and selected browser. The game does not request contacts, microphone, camera, precise location or photo-library access for gameplay.

Sharing, security and retention

Advertising and store providers receive information directly when their services are used; their processing and retention are governed by their policies. Information may be processed outside your country. CampStudio does not operate a player-account database for this game or sell save files. Local data remains until removed or overwritten; provider records may be retained for security, financial or legal purposes. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can play offline, decline optional advertising, change privacy choices, manage notification permissions, restore eligible purchases and manage subscriptions in your store account. Removing local app data removes local saves but does not cancel subscriptions or delete store transaction records. Depending on your location, you may have access, correction, deletion, objection or other privacy rights. Contact us at the address above; for data controlled by Google or Apple, use their privacy controls or contact them directly.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us. Where a higher age of consent applies, follow local requirements and seek a parent or guardian's guidance.

Changes

We may update this policy when game features or service providers change. The latest version and update date are published here.
